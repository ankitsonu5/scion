About this item

  • Scent Profile: Zesty bitter orange and lemon open this fruity floral gourmand, flowing into a heart of rose, heliotrope, and orange blossom, resting on a warm, indulgent base of vanilla, almond, and sandalwood.
  • Long Lasting Perfume: High concentration of perfume oils ensures a captivating scent that lasts up to 12 hours — perfect as a unisex signature fragrance for daily wear or special occasions.
  • Pure Essential & Aromatherapy Oils: Infused with natural essential oils including mandarin, orange, patchouli, and vanilla resin, offering a richer, authentic aroma that delights the senses.
  • Ethically Made in Dubai: Proudly cruelty-free and vegan, reflecting Maison de l’Avenir’s commitment to ethical luxury and conscious beauty.
  • Ideal Gift for Eid & Special Occasions: A sensual and addictive perfume for men and women, ideal as a women’s perfume, men’s perfume, or thoughtful gift for Eid, birthdays, or anniversaries.

Top Notes

Bitter Orange, Lemon, Cotton Candy, Melon, Jasmine, Bergamot, Cassis, Pineapple, Orange

Heart Notes

Base Notes

Almond, Sandalwood, Cashmere Wood, Patchouli, Chocolate, Vanilla, Amber, Musk

Jardin De Jade is a luxurious unisex fruity floral gourmand eau de parfum for men and women who love uplifting, playful, and velvety scents. Bright notes of bitter orange and lemon lead into a heart of rose, heliotrope, and orange blossom, while a warm base of vanilla, almond, and sandalwood wraps the fragrance in cozy indulgence. Infused with natural perfume and aromatherapy oils, this long lasting perfume, proudly made in Dubai and cruelty-free, delivers an irresistible signature scent — perfect as a gift for Eid or any special occasion.
