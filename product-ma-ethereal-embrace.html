Ethereal Embrace is a luxurious unisex fruity floral gourmand eau de parfum designed for men and women who adore sweet, velvety, and uplifting scents. Opening with zesty mandarin and grapefruit, it transitions into a heart of raspberry, black currant, orange blossom, and lily, settling into a warm, indulgent base of marshmallow, vanilla, and amber. Infused with natural perfume and aromatherapy oils, this long lasting perfume for men and women, proudly made in Dubai and cruelty-free, creates an irresistible signature fragrance — perfect for gifting on Eid, birthdays, or any special occasion.
